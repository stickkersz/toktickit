# TokTickIT

An IT service desk where staff of an organisation report technology problems and an IT team works them to resolution. This glossary fixes the vocabulary used across the specifications, the code, and the database, so one concept never travels under two names.

## People and access

**User**:
An account that can sign in. Every User holds exactly one Role.
_Avoid_: account holder, member, RequesterUser

**Role**:
The single permission set attached to a User: Requester, IT Staff, or Administrator.
_Avoid_: permission, group, access level

**Requester**:
A User who reports problems and owns the Tickets they submit. Also the role name for that User.
_Avoid_: reporter, customer, end user, Development Requester

**IT Staff**:
A User who works Tickets: takes ownership, prioritises internally, and moves a Ticket through its workflow.
_Avoid_: agent, technician, support staff, IT Support

**Administrator**:
A User who manages accounts: creating them, editing them, assigning a Role, and activating or deactivating them.
_Avoid_: admin user, superuser, owner

**Session**:
A signed-in period established by a successful login and ended by logout or expiry.
_Avoid_: login, token, auth state

**Initial Password**:
A password issued by an Administrator that its holder must replace before reaching the application.
_Avoid_: temporary password, default password, reset password

**Active**:
Whether an account may currently be used. Deactivation is the only removal mechanism; Users are never deleted.
_Avoid_: enabled, archived, deleted

## Tickets

**Ticket**:
One reported problem, owned for its whole life by the Requester who submitted it.
_Avoid_: case, request, issue, incident

**Ticket Number**:
The human-facing identifier of a Ticket, generated by the backend and never reused.
_Avoid_: reference, ID, case number

**Ticket Owner**:
The single IT Staff or Administrator User currently responsible for working a Ticket. A Ticket may have none.
_Avoid_: assignee, handler, responsible party

**Ineligible Owner**:
A Ticket Owner who has since been deactivated or is no longer IT Staff or an Administrator. The Ticket keeps naming them, is treated as having no owner for claiming and for moving to In Progress, Resolved, or Closed, and is listed under Needs an owner until someone takes it over. Derived at read time, never stored.
_Avoid_: orphaned ticket, stale owner

**Requested Priority**:
The urgency the Requester selected when submitting. Fixed at creation and never changed afterwards.
_Avoid_: user priority, reported priority

**IT Priority**:
The urgency IT assigns internally. Starts as a copy of the Requested Priority and may then diverge from it.
_Avoid_: real priority, internal priority, staff priority

**Ticket Status**:
Where a Ticket sits in its workflow. Only IT Staff and Administrators change it.
_Avoid_: state, stage, phase

**Resolution Summary**:
The explanation of how a Ticket was resolved, written by IT and visible to the Requester.
_Avoid_: resolution note, closing comment, fix description

**Problem Appears Resolved**:
The Requester's indication that their problem seems fixed. It is a signal to IT, not a status change.
_Avoid_: requester close, mark as resolved, confirm resolution

**Category**:
The kind of problem a Ticket reports, chosen from a maintained list.
_Avoid_: type, classification, topic

**Related System**:
The service or equipment a Ticket concerns, chosen from a maintained list.
_Avoid_: affected system, asset, component

## Communication and evidence

**Public Comment**:
A message on a Ticket visible to its Requester and to all IT Staff and Administrators. Append only.
_Avoid_: comment, reply, message, conversation

**Internal Note**:
A message on a Ticket visible only to IT Staff and Administrators, never to the Requester. Append only.
_Avoid_: private comment, staff note, internal comment

**Attachment**:
A file a Requester adds to their Ticket as evidence. IT Staff and Administrators can read and download it but never add or remove one.
_Avoid_: file, upload, document

**Removed Attachment**:
An Attachment withdrawn with a stated reason. Its metadata stays visible; its contents become unreachable.
_Avoid_: deleted attachment, archived file
